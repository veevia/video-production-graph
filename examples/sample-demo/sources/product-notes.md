# Product notes — Northwind Water Monitor (fictional, synthetic data)

- The monitor compares each plant's daily water use with that plant's own expected range and raises a **water-use signal** when use leaves the range.
- The **expected range** is fitted per plant from its own history.
- The **seven-day outlook** is a planned range derived from the production plan. It is *not* a statistical forecast, so never say the product "predicts".
- The dashboard's home view lists plants; opening a plant shows its signal, its expected range and the outlook card.
- All data in this example is synthetic.
